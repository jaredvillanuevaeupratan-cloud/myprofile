About CarePlus Hospital

Learn more about our mission, values, and commitment to excellence in healthcare.

30+ Years of Medical Excellence

Founded in 1994, CarePlus Hospital has been at the forefront of medical innovation and patient care for over three decades. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide accessible, high-quality healthcare to our community.

Today, we've grown into a premier medical facility equipped with modern technology and staffed by some of the region's most skilled healthcare professionals. We believe in treating not just the illness, but the whole person - body, mind, and spirit.

Our commitment extends beyond treatment to prevention and education, empowering patients to take control of their health and live fuller, healthier lives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and procedures.

Do you accept insurance? +

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans including Medicare, Medicaid, and private insurance. Please contact our billing department at (555) 123-4567 to verify your specific coverage.

How do I schedule an appointment? +

You can schedule an appointment by calling our main line at (555) 123-4567, using our online booking system, or visiting our reception desk. For urgent matters, we offer same-day appointments.

What are your visiting hours? +

General visiting hours are from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. However, visiting hours may vary by department. ICU visiting hours are limited to 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM.

Do you offer emergency services? +

Yes, our Emergency Department is open 24/7, 365 days a year. We have a dedicated team of emergency physicians and nurses ready to handle all types of medical emergencies.

Can I get my test results online? +

Yes! We offer a secure patient portal where you can view your test results, medical records, and communicate with your healthcare providers. Ask our staff for registration details.
